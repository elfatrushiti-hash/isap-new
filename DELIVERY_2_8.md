# Delivery 2.8 - Intrum Knowledge Taxonomy

Extends the local Rule Intelligence Core with an administrable knowledge taxonomy.

## Included
- Knowledge items for products, pain points, buying signals, objections, industries, topics, questions and content
- Editable relationships between knowledge items
- Knowledge graph statistics and orphan detection
- Persistent storage in the existing ISAP intelligence knowledge model
- Rule mode remains local and deterministic
- External AI remains locked and no new outbound network communication is introduced
